### 9. Fix Low Contrast / Dim Text Colors Across App
- **Scope:** Global UI (Badges, Leaderboard, Calendar, Book Details, Dashboard)
- **Issue:** Various dynamic metadata labels (dates, completion status badges, secondary attributes, percentages, and legends) use low-contrast/dim colors, rendering them unreadable on dark backgrounds.
- **Action:** Update all secondary text, meta labels, status badges, and dynamic percentages across components to use high-contrast, accessible light colors (e.g., accessible light greens, bright grays `#94A3B8`/`#E2E8F0`).
